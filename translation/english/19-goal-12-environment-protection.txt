[0641] Environment protection
[0642] Protected reefs and wetlands, and a circular economy
[0643] Addu City will protect and restore its unique natural environment while building a climate-resilient circular economy that supports sustainable growth and community wellbeing. The city will conserve coral reefs, wetlands, mangroves, beaches, and other ecologically significant ecosystems by designating and effectively managing protected areas and nature parks, restoring degraded habitats, and promoting responsible public access and eco-tourism. Through resource efficiency, waste reduction, recycling, nature-based solutions, and climate-resilient infrastructure, Addu will enhance biodiversity, strengthen resilience to climate change, and create a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable city for present and future generations.
